# Internal support playbook (synthetic)

> Internal document written for this challenge to simulate the first-level support knowledge an operations team would maintain. It is not Getnet content; answers based on it must be presented as general guidance.

## Card machine does not connect to the internet

1. Check the status bar of the card machine: the Wi-Fi or 4G icon shows whether there is signal.
2. Restart the card machine: hold the power button until it turns off, wait 10 seconds and turn it on again.
3. If you use Wi-Fi, check that the router is on and that other devices can browse. Reconfigure the network in the machine settings if the password changed.
4. If you use the 4G chip, move the machine closer to a window or open area; metal counters and basements reduce signal.
5. Switch the connection mode (Wi-Fi ↔ 4G chip) to check whether the problem is the network or the machine.
6. Make sure the battery is above 15%; some machines disable connectivity in power-saving mode.
7. If the machine stays offline after these steps, open a support ticket with the serial number so a technician can check the device or send a replacement.

## Transaction declined at the card machine

- A decline usually comes from the card issuer (the customer's bank), not from the machine.
- Read the decline code shown on the screen or receipt. Common codes: 05 not authorized by the issuer, 51 insufficient funds, 55 incorrect PIN, 54 expired card, 57 transaction not permitted for the card, 91 issuer unavailable.
- Ask the customer to try another card or another payment method (debit, Pix QR Code).
- If every card is declined, the problem may be the machine connection or registration: restart it and open a support ticket.

## Settlement questions

- Sales are deposited according to the merchant's plan (for example, "receive in 1 business day").
- Settlements happen on business days; sales made on Friday with D+1 are deposited on Monday.
- Amounts in the statement are gross; the plan fees are discounted at settlement.
